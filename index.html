FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit. Can't find an answer? Get in touch and we'll be happy to help.

How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend a check-up and professional cleaning every six months. Regular visits help us catch small issues early, keeping your teeth and gums healthy and preventing costly treatments down the line.

Is the dental treatment painful?

Not at all — we use modern, gentle techniques and anesthesia where needed, so most treatments are virtually pain-free. We always check in with you throughout to keep you comfortable.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes, we work with most major insurance providers. Bring your policy details to your first visit and our front desk team will help verify your coverage.

How can I book an appointment?

You can book online through our appointment form above, call either branch directly, or send us a message — our team confirms every request within 24 hours.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call either branch right away — we hold same-day emergency slots for urgent issues like severe pain, swelling, or a knocked-out tooth.

Is teeth whitening safe?

Yes, professional in-clinic whitening is safe and closely monitored by our specialists, unlike over-the-counter kits which can be harsher on enamel and gums.
